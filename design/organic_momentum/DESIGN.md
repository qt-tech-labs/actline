## Brand & Style

This design system embodies grounded momentum, cognitive calm, and purposeful execution. Designed for people navigating multi-phase plans, creative production, and daily habit execution, the interface strips away performative task management in favor of physical tactility and organic clarity.

The visual identity sits at the intersection of **Tactile Craft** and **Modern Ergonomics**. It evokes high-grade Japanese stationery, precision bench tools, and physical workshop journals:
- **Atmosphere**: Grounded, peaceful, and sharp. It trades cold, clinical SaaS minimalism for warm paper stocks, debossed containers, and crisp stone geometry.
- **Cognitive Clarity**: Action lists are never cluttered walls of text. Every task is an intentional, self-contained workshop unit equipped with context, duration, and underlying rationale.
- **Motion & Feedback**: Interaction states respond with weighted resistance—snappy, tactile toggle transitions and physical completion states that make progress feel earned and material.

## Colors

The color palette grounds digital productivity in botanical resilience and natural materials:

- **Primary (`#2D5A46` — Deep Forest Sage)**: Anchors deliberate focus, milestone completion, and verified states. Used for primary execution targets, key progress indicators, and active milestones.
- **Secondary (`#D97736` — Warm Amber Clay)**: Communicates advance preparation, required provisions, critical timing, and alert states without triggering anxiety or panic.
- **Tertiary (`#475569` — Warm Slate)**: Governs operational structure, timelines, structural rules, metadata, and step-by-step guidance.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F9F8F5` — Crisp Linen Off-White)**: Replaces sterile blue-light white with a soft, warm paper canvas that prevents eye strain during prolonged planning sessions.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional cadence between architectural planning and human execution:

- **Headlines (Space Grotesk)**: Architectural, proportional, and clean. Its technical character brings momentum and precision to milestone titles, phase headers, and daily targets.
- **Body & Structural Text (Manrope)**: Highly legible, open humanist geometry that maintains comfort across dense execution lists, micro-steps, and contextual rationale descriptions.
- **Metrics, Durations & Badges (JetBrains Mono)**: Monospaced numerical clarity for time tracking, checklist counters, quantity requirements, and timestamp verification tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a hybrid fixed-fluid ergonomic canvas that balances continuous context with granular execution.

### Grid Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: Asymmetric 12-column layout. 
  - Left/Top sticky anchor (4 columns or top full-width band) for active project switching, milestone progress track, and prep checklists.
  - Core workstream (8 columns, max-width 840px) focused on the current day's execution flow.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 24px margins. Milestone progress pins to top header; prep list and actions stack vertically.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Single column stream with 16px lateral padding. Sticky header collapses into a condensed milestone meter with quick swipe project selector.

### Rhythm & Proximity Rules
- High vertical density inside task sub-blocks (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) pairs related steps closely to their parent action.
- Distinct task blocks are separated by `space-lg` to create distinct visual pause points, avoiding the illusion of infinite work.
- Structural layout modules (e.g., separating Supplies from Daily Actions) are spaced via `space-xl` or `space-2xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system eschews floating SaaS drop-shadows in favor of **Tonal Paper Layering** and **Subtle Debossed Insets**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas)**: Tinted warm linen (`#F9F8F5`), fully flat.
- **Surface Level 1 (Work Cards, Panels)**: Pure crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px boundary stroke (`#E5E0D8`) and a light micro-shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(30, 38, 33, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(30, 38, 33, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Nested Steps, Micro-toolbars, Rationale Blocks)**: Tinted stone trays (`#F2EFE9`) nested directly within cards, framed by subtle hairline dividers (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`), creating a physical carved-out appearance.
- **Active / Drag State**: Lifted elevation via `0 8px 24px rgba(45, 90, 70, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`, accompanied by a slight scale increase (1.008x) to simulate picking up a physical planner card.

## Shapes

A balanced `0.5rem` (`8px`) baseline roundedness keeps the experience structured yet organic:

- **Base Cards & Modules**: `0.75rem` (`12px`) outer radius provides a smooth, notebook-like edge.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges**: `0.5rem` (`8px`) for buttons, `0.375rem` (`6px`) for mono duration tags.
- **Timeline Nodes & Switcher Pills**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) for milestone nodes, progress meters, and dynamic filters.
- **Proof Image Attachments**: Uniform `0.5rem` (`8px`) clipping mask with a 1px interior inset border (`inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`).

## Components

### Project Switcher & Header Bar
- **Container**: Fixed top glass-linen bar with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and subtle bottom border `#E5E0D8`.
- **Active Project Selector**: Compact trigger showing the current project name, an active phase badge, and an earthy color pip. On click, expands a card showing active timelines, completion percentages, and rapid switching.

### Milestone Timeline Tracker
- Horizontal step rail with solid track lines (`#E5E0D8`) and active progression fill (`#2D5A46`).
- Uncompleted milestones appear as hollow warm slate rings; completed milestones display as solid forest sage nodes with checkmarks; the active milestone is an amber clay halo ring pulsing gently.

### Daily Action Item & Checklist
- **Card**: White surface, 1px border.
- **Checkbox**: `20x20px` tactile square with a `4px` corner radius. In default state, border is `#D0C9BD`. When checked, transforms into solid `#2D5A46` with an animated mechanical tick mark. Completed task text transitions to `#87928A` with a soft strike-through line.
- **Duration Badge**: Rendered in `label-sm` font with a faint `#F2EFE9` background and `#475569` text (e.g., `⏱ 25m`).
- **Rationale ('Why') Tray**: Collapsible or sub-indented section styled in `#F2EFE9` with a left border accent (`2px solid #2D5A46`). Uses `body-sm` font in italic or muted tone to communicate purpose without cognitive clutter.

### Step-by-Step Micro-Steps
- Numbered micro-rail (`01`, `02`, `03`) in `label-sm` monospace.
- Individual check off lines allow partial progression of larger daily tasks.

### Supplies & Advance Preparation Checklist
- Bordered auxiliary panel with an amber status badge (`PREP REQUIRED`).
- Check items include quantity badges (`JetBrains Mono`, bold) and advance-notice tags (`e.g., Buy 1 day prior`).
- Includes an quick-add field for ad-hoc tool or supply reminders.

### Photo Proof & Completion Check-In
- Dropzone and verified preview tile (`80x80px` on mobile, `110x110px` on desktop) with an inset border.
- States: Empty (dashed border with camera glyph), Uploading (circular progress indicator), and Completed (vivid thumbnail with sage green check pill overlay).

### Buttons & Inputs
- **Primary Button**: Solid Sage (`#2D5A46`), white text, crisp inner micro-bevel. Hover shifts to `#234737`.
- **Secondary / Action Button**: Off-white paper background, `#1E2621` text, 1px `#D0C9BD` border.
- **Input Fields**: Crisp off-white interior (`#F9F8F5`), 1px border (`#E5E0D8`), transitioning to a 2px `#2D5A46` focus ring without heavy outer glow.
